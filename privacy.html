Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how BL.co ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit bl.co, interact with any BL.co division's website, or use a product built under the BL.co ecosystem. It applies across Software, Web, Labs, Webtastic Games, and Media unless a specific product states otherwise.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • Information you provide directly — through contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, or project inquiries, including your name, email, company, and message content.
  • Information collected automatically — such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and referral source, gathered through standard analytics tooling.
  • Information from product use — where a specific BL.co product requires an account or stores usage data, that product's own notice will describe what's collected.

How We Use It

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, operate and improve our websites and products, understand aggregate usage patterns, send updates to newsletter subscribers who've opted in, and meet legal or security obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies & Tracking

BL.co websites use a limited set of cookies for essential site functionality and basic, privacy-respecting analytics. See our Cookie Policy for the full breakdown of what's used and why.

Data Sharing

We don't share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Limited data may be shared with service providers who help us operate our sites (such as hosting or email delivery), under obligations to keep it confidential, or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain contact and inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain reasonable business records, and delete or anonymize it when it's no longer needed for that purpose.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email we send. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

BL.co's websites and products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as BL.co and its divisions grow. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@bl.co.
