FAQ

Before you write in.

What kind of projects does BL.co take on?
Anything that fits one of our five divisions and has a clear reason to exist — software products, web builds, browser games, or media projects. We're more interested in a well-defined problem than a big budget.
How long does a typical project take?
It depends entirely on scope. A focused web build can move in a few weeks; a full SaaS product or game usually runs a few months. We'll give you a real timeline after the first conversation, not before.
Do you work with early-stage founders?
Yes — some of our best collaborations have started with a founder who had a clear problem and no deck. What matters more than stage is whether the idea has a specific reason to exist.
Can I work with a specific division directly?
Yes. Mention the division — Software, Web, Labs, Webtastic Games, or Media — in your message and we'll route it straight there.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance after launch?
For ventures built under BL.co, yes — long-term maintenance is part of how we think about a launch. For external client work, it's scoped separately depending on what you need.
What's the difference between a client project and joining the ecosystem?
A client project is scoped, delivered, and handed off. Joining the ecosystem means your venture becomes one of our divisions' products — sharing infrastructure and design language, with BL.co staying involved long-term.
Is BL.co hiring?
Not actively, but we keep a running list of people we'd want to work with. Check the Careers page for the current status.
