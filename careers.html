Culture

How we actually work.

Small by Design

Five divisions, run lean. Every collaborator owns real scope — nobody's shipping tickets for someone else's roadmap.

Async First

Remote-first and mostly asynchronous. Meetings happen when they earn their place on the calendar, not by default.

Direct Feedback

Flat structure. Notes on your work come straight from whoever's reviewing it — no relay through layers of management.

Why Join

What working here actually gets you.

Ownership Over Tickets

You own a division's outcome, not a backlog someone else prioritized.

Cross-Division Exposure

Work touches more than one division when it makes sense — rare in most single-product teams.

Flexible, Remote-First

Work from wherever, on hours that overlap enough to collaborate — not a fixed shift.

Direct Access to Direction

No layers between you and the person setting the standard for the work.

Open Roles

Current status.

No Open Roles Right Now

BL.co is small enough that we bring people in project by project rather than posting roles ahead of need. If that changes, it'll be listed here first.

Hiring Process

What happens if you reach out.

01

Intro Conversation

A short, informal call — what you've built, what you're looking for, and whether there's an obvious fit.

02

Working Session

A real, paid task close to actual work — not a take-home puzzle disconnected from what you'd do here.

03

Fit Conversation

A conversation about how you work day-to-day — pace, communication, ownership — not another skills test.

04

Decision

A direct answer, quickly — either a place on a project or honest, specific feedback either way.
